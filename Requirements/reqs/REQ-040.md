# REQ-040 — Puzzle difficulty and order

## Statement
The game SHALL list puzzles by kind (mini, then warm-up, then full), then by their difficulty rating, lowest first, and by id within a rating.

## Rationale
A gentle start: SRC-008 asks for an easy start.

## Rules
- Rating: an integer from 1 to 5, shown as dots in the top bar.
- Kind: the puzzle file's `kind` field (mini, warmup, full). (SRC-011, F7)

## Acceptance
- **A1** — The first puzzle shown on a fresh install has rating 1.
- **A2** — A full puzzle of rating 1 whose id sorts before "shapes-warmup" still comes after every warm-up.

## Out of scope
Player-chosen sorting.

- none
