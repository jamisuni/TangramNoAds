# REQ-039 — A picture for every puzzle

## Statement
The game SHALL include, for every puzzle, a stylised picture that is drawn inside the puzzle's outline only.

## Rationale
SRC-002: the solved view shows "a picture … with exact same frame".

## Rules
- The picture uses flat shapes, 3–8 colours and no text. *(Inferred from SRC-004)*
- A puzzle is released only after a person has solved it and approved its picture (reviewedByHuman: true).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — No picture paints outside its puzzle's outline.
- **A2** — No released puzzle has reviewedByHuman: false.
