# REQ-032 — Settings from the top bar

## Statement
WHEN the player presses the gear in the top bar, the game SHALL open the settings screen over the current puzzle, and WHEN it is closed, the game SHALL return to the same puzzle state.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "maybe settings menu on top" (the owner's idea). The difficulty choice left the screen with the levels (REQ-027 withdrawn, SRC-011).

## Rules
- Contents, in this order: timer (REQ-031), sound (REQ-033), play time and best times (REQ-029, REQ-030), reset (REQ-034), a short how-to-play text, the free note (REQ-009) and the privacy text (REQ-049).
- Puzzle time is paused while the settings screen is open.
- The Android back button closes the settings screen. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Opening and closing the settings changes nothing on the board.
- **A2** — No difficulty control is present.

## Out of scope
A parent gate: nothing in the settings costs money or leaves the device.
