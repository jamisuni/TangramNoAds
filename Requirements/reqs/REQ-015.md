# REQ-015 — Tap or drag, and touch areas

## Statement
WHEN a touch on a piece ends having moved less than 12 dp, the game SHALL treat it as a tap regardless of its duration; otherwise the game SHALL treat it as a drag.

## Rationale
Research on children's touch input (SRC-005) shows long holds and imprecise taps; a movement-based rule avoids accidental drags and turns.

## Rules
- The tap threshold is 12 dp of movement.
- The touch area of a board piece is its shape grown by 12 dp; the touch area of a tray piece is its whole tray cell.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A 3 s still press on a piece acts as a tap.
- **A2** — A touch 10 dp outside a small triangle's edge picks it up.

## Out of scope
Multi-touch gestures (REQ-017).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the 12 dp threshold and the 12 dp touch-area margin.
