# REQ-030 — Puzzle solve time and best time

## Statement
WHILE an unsolved puzzle that the player has touched is shown, the game SHALL count active seconds as that puzzle's time, and WHEN the puzzle is solved, the game SHALL store the time and keep the shortest as the best time.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "keep track of per puzzle time used to solve".

## Rules
- The time pauses while another puzzle or the settings screen is shown, and resumes on return.
- Restart and Retry reset the running time, not the best time.
- The best time is shown on the solved view (REQ-026) and in the settings list. *(Inferred from SRC-004)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Browsing away for 2 minutes adds nothing to the puzzle's time.
- **A2** — Solving again slower keeps the earlier, faster best time.
