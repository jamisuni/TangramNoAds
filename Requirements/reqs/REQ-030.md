# REQ-030 — Puzzle solve time and best time

## Statement
WHILE a puzzle in progress (TYPE-006) is shown, the game SHALL count active seconds as that puzzle's time, and WHEN the puzzle is solved, the game SHALL store the time and keep the shortest as the best time.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "keep track of per puzzle time used to solve". A puzzle becomes In progress when a piece first leaves the tray (TYPE-006, SRC-011 F8), so browsing past a puzzle or turning a piece in the tray costs nothing.

## Rules
- The time pauses while another puzzle or the settings screen is shown, and resumes on return.
- Restart and Retry reset the running time, not the best time.
- The best time is shown on the solved view (REQ-026) and in the settings list. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*
- A puzzle solved by the developer aid (REQ-046) sets no best time.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Browsing away for 2 minutes adds nothing to the puzzle's time.
- **A2** — Solving again slower keeps the earlier, faster best time.
- **A3** — Turning a tray piece on a New puzzle starts no time.
