# REQ-002 — Classic tangram for school-age players

## Statement
The game SHALL let a player aged 8 or older rebuild a shown silhouette from the classic seven tangram pieces by dragging, turning and dropping them.

## Rationale
SRC-001: "traditional Android Tangram game", "drag and drop them to shaped area", "highly easy to use". SRC-002: "targeted to 8 years and older", "no toddler modes", "We dont make it too easy".

## Rules
- The piece set is TYPE-001.
- The target audience is 8 years and older; there are no modes for younger children.
- The challenge is in solving the puzzle; the controls themselves must not be the difficulty.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Every puzzle can be completed with pieces of TYPE-001 by drag, turn and drop only.
- **A2** — No screen offers a toddler or picture-matching mode.

## Out of scope
Non-traditional piece sets (idea #PieceSets). Toddler modes (SRC-002: none).
