# REQ-011 — Empty silhouette board

## Statement
WHILE a puzzle is unsolved, the game SHALL show its target as a single flat silhouette with no inner lines, slot outlines or other hints of where pieces go.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "Places where piece can be dropped shouldnt be show ahead of time but puzzle layout is empty".

## Rules
- One colour for the whole silhouette.
- Placed pieces are drawn on top of the silhouette in their own colours.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — No inner line or slot outline is visible on an unsolved puzzle.
- **A2** — The silhouette outline matches the puzzle's solution outline exactly.

## Out of scope
Hint modes (none are planned).
