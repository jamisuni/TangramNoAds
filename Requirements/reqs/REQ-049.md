# REQ-049 — Privacy text in the settings

## Statement
WHEN the settings screen is open, the game SHALL show the privacy policy as a short text below the free note (REQ-009), in the game's language (REQ-047).

## Rationale
The store wants "a privacy policy link or text within the app itself" (SRC-012); a text needs no network and no permission. The owner accepted it (SRC-011, G2).

## Rules
- The text says, in full: "Privacy: this game collects no data. It has no network access, no accounts and no analytics. Your puzzle progress and play times are stored only on this device and are deleted when the game is uninstalled." *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-012: the wording is the agent's)*
- The text is plain text, not a link.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — The settings screen shows the privacy text in full, below the free note.

## Out of scope
The hosted copy for the store's field (REQ-048).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the wording above, and its Finnish translation in the prototype.
