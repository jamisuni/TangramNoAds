# REQ-043 — Size marks on the tray triangles

## Statement
WHILE a triangle is in the tray, the game SHALL show in its tray cell a mark of its size class: S for a small triangle, M for the medium triangle and L for a large triangle.

## Rationale
SRC-009: "images on bottom should some level indicate size they going to be.. so maybe like small, medium, big ones from same component". The three triangles share one shape, so their miniatures are the easiest pieces to mix up.

## Rules
- The mark sits in the top-left corner of the cell, tinted with the piece's colour, and is hidden while the piece is on the board.
- The square and the parallelogram get no mark: each shape exists in one size only.
- The mark never changes the piece's size (REQ-014) or the tray scale (REQ-013).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A full-set tray shows the marks L, L, M, S, S on the five triangles, and none on the square or the parallelogram.
- **A2** — A placed triangle's cell shows only its dashed outline, without a mark.

## Out of scope
Size marks for other piece sets (idea #PieceSets).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: letters S, M, L are understood by players aged 8+; SRC-009 names small, medium and big. The marks stay (SRC-011, G1).
- ASSUMPTION: the letters S, M, L are used in both languages (REQ-047); Finnish size labels use the same letters.
