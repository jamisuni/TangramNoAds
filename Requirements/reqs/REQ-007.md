# REQ-007 — Puzzle library

## Statement
The game SHALL ship a library of puzzles, each built from the full piece set (mini puzzles excepted), with a stylised picture, and SHALL start with mini and warm-up puzzles.

## Rationale
SRC-002: solved picture "with exact same frame". SRC-003: no hidden lock points (a runtime rule, REQ-019; the "gift box" content restriction of round 2 was reversed in round 6, SRC-011). SRC-008: "add there few very easy ones to get easy start"; "puzzles dont have to be anything … even rectangle is fine".

## Rules
- Puzzles are held in their own folder, separate from the requirements (SRC-008).
- A puzzle may depict an object or be an abstract shape.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Every shipped puzzle meets REQ-038, REQ-039 and REQ-040.
- **A2** — The first puzzles in the list are the mini puzzles (REQ-045), then the warm-ups (REQ-041).

## Out of scope
Puzzle editor for players; downloadable puzzle packs.
