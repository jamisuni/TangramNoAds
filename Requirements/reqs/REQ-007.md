# REQ-007 — Puzzle library

## Statement
The game SHALL ship a library of puzzles, each built from the full piece set, buildable from its edges inwards, with a stylised picture, and SHALL start with warm-up puzzles.

## Rationale
SRC-002: solved picture "with exact same frame". SRC-003: no support for pieces without neighbours or corners ("we do not support gift boxes"). SRC-008: "add there few very easy ones to get easy start"; "puzzles dont have to be anything … even rectangle is fine".

## Rules
- Puzzles are held in their own folder, separate from the requirements (SRC-008).
- A puzzle may depict an object or be an abstract shape.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Every shipped puzzle meets REQ-038, REQ-039 and REQ-040.
- **A2** — The first puzzles in the list are warm-ups (REQ-041).

## Out of scope
Puzzle editor for players; downloadable puzzle packs.
