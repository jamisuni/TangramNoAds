# REQ-044 — Turn buttons in the tray

> **Withdrawn 2026-09-28 (SRC-011).** The owner dropped the tray ↺ ↻ buttons: "didnt realize earlier I can just press it and it spins", "pressing is enough". Tapping a tray piece (REQ-016) is the way to turn it before dragging. The 26 dp button strip leaves the tray cells (REQ-013).

## Statement
WHILE a piece is in the tray, the game SHALL show a turn-left (↺) and a turn-right (↻) button in its tray cell, and WHEN the player presses one, the game SHALL turn that piece one 45° step of TYPE-003 in that direction and keep the turn when the piece is dragged out.

## Rationale
SRC-009: "maybe components under there has little spin icons inside of boxes to allow rotating them ready on bottom there before pulling them position". With a mouse there is no second finger for the twist, and tapping is only one direction; the buttons turn either way.

## Rules
- The buttons sit in a strip at the bottom of the cell, below the piece, and never cover it.
- Each button has a visible diameter of at least 17 dp and a touch area at least 10 dp larger.
- Pressing a button never starts a drag.
- The buttons are hidden while the piece is on the board.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — One ↻ press turns a tray piece 45° clockwise; one ↺ press turns it back.
- **A2** — A piece turned in the tray and then dragged to the board keeps that turn during the drag.

## Out of scope
Turning a piece on the board (REQ-016, REQ-017).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the buttons show on every device, touch screens included, until the owner's phone playtest decides. SRC-009: "at least temporary"; SRC-010: "maybe those ↺ ↻ are not long run not need as pressing does same... but we keep them now".
