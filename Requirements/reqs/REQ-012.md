# REQ-012 — The puzzle's pieces in a fixed tray

## Statement
WHILE a puzzle is unsolved, the game SHALL show every piece of the puzzle that is not on the board as a miniature in the tray below the board, in the order, colours and resting turns of TYPE-001.

## Rationale
SRC-001: "set of miniatyre blocks is bottom of screen". SRC-002: "maybe even present pieces always specific standard order on bottom" and "blocks can be different colors" (the owner's ideas).

## Rules
- Tray order, colours and resting turns: TYPE-001.
- A piece on the board leaves a dashed outline in its tray cell.
- A piece dropped back on the tray returns to its own cell.
- A mini puzzle (REQ-045) shows only its own pieces, in the same relative order.
- The puzzle's title is shown in the game's language (REQ-047).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Two different full-set puzzles show identical trays at their start.
- **A2** — Every piece is always either in the tray, on the board, or being dragged.

## Out of scope
Tray layout sizes (REQ-013, REQ-035, REQ-036).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the fixed order and resting turns are adopted as the rule (the owner said "maybe").
