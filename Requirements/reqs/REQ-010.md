# REQ-010 — Everything stays on the device

## Statement
The game SHALL work without any network connection and SHALL NOT send any data off the device.

## Rationale
Keeps the game free of accounts and tracking, and makes the Google Play Families data rules (SRC-007) trivially met.

## Rules
- Progress, settings and play time are stored only on the device.
- The game requests no Android permissions.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — With the device in airplane mode, every feature works.
- **A2** — The installed app declares no permissions, not even network access.

## Out of scope
Backup or sync between devices.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: no network use at all. The owner never asked for it; this is the agent's reading of "absolute free" and "NoAds".

- none
