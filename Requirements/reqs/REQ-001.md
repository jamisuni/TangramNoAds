# REQ-001 — Enjoy, it's absolutely free

## Statement
The game SHALL be completely free to enjoy: it SHALL NOT contain advertising, SHALL NOT sell anything, and SHALL NOT ask the player or a parent for money in any form.

## Rationale
The owner's theme: "enjoy, its absolute free" (SRC-003); "No money making, no adds, just enjoy freely" (SRC-002). Nobody needs income from this game.

## Rules
- No advertising of any kind, including the developer's own promotions.
- No in-app purchases, subscriptions, tips or donations.
- No content is locked behind payment, time or ads.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A full play-through of every screen shows no ad, no price and no request for money.
- **A2** — The store listing does not carry the "Contains ads" or "In-app purchases" labels.

## Out of scope
How the project is funded (not needed: SRC-003).
