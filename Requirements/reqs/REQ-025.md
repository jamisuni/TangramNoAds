# REQ-025 — Continue or restart a half-done puzzle

## Statement
WHEN the player returns to a puzzle in progress, the game SHALL show it exactly as it was left, and WHEN the player presses Restart, the game SHALL return all its pieces to the tray and reset its time.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "If leaving puzzle half way can return it and restart if so wants or continue".

## Rules
- Saved per piece: tray or board, position, turn, mirror.
- Restart is shown only while the puzzle is in progress.
- The state survives closing the app. *(Inferred from SRC-004)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Leave a puzzle with three pieces placed, come back: the same three pieces are in the same places.
- **A2** — After Restart, all the puzzle's pieces are in the tray and the puzzle time is zero.

## Out of scope
Undo of single moves.
