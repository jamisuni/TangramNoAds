# REQ-018 — Mirror the parallelogram

## Statement
WHERE the difficulty is Hard, the game SHALL show a flip button next to the parallelogram and SHALL mirror the parallelogram when it is pressed; WHERE the difficulty is Easy or Medium, the game SHALL choose the parallelogram's mirror image automatically when it locks.

## Rationale
The parallelogram is the only piece whose mirror image differs (TYPE-003); classic tangram lets it be turned over.

## Rules
- The flip button's touch area is at least 60 dp across.
- On the board, a mirror is kept only if the mirrored piece still locks in place (as REQ-016).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — On Hard, the parallelogram can be mirrored in the tray and on the board.
- **A2** — On Easy and Medium, no flip button is shown.

## Out of scope
Mirroring the other pieces (no visible effect).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: mirroring is part of the game, automatic on Easy and Medium and by button on Hard. The owner asked whether a mirror button is needed (SRC-010, a question); the agent explained this split and the owner raised no objection.
