# REQ-018 — Mirror the parallelogram

## Statement
WHILE the parallelogram is in the tray or on the board, the game SHALL show a flip badge (⇋) next to it, and WHEN the player presses the badge, the game SHALL mirror the parallelogram.

## Rationale
The parallelogram is the only piece whose mirror image differs (TYPE-003); classic tangram lets it be turned over, and learning to flip is part of the game. The owner asked for a mirror button (SRC-010) and accepted that the badge shows always (SRC-011, D2). There is no automatic mirroring: the levels that had it were removed (REQ-004 withdrawn).

## Rules
- The badge's touch area is at least 60 dp across (REQ-037).
- On the board, a mirror is kept only if the mirrored piece still locks in place (as REQ-016); otherwise it mirrors back with a short shake.
- The lock search never tries the other mirror image by itself.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — The parallelogram can be mirrored in the tray and on the board.
- **A2** — The badge is visible in every puzzle that contains the parallelogram, and only next to the parallelogram.
- **A3** — A parallelogram dropped in its mirror image of a fitting spot does not lock there.

## Out of scope
Mirroring the other pieces (no visible effect).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: 60 dp touch area and the ⇋ symbol (prototype 0.3).
