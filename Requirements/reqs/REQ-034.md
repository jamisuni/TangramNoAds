# REQ-034 — Reset all progress

## Statement
WHEN the player confirms Reset all progress in the settings, the game SHALL set every puzzle to New and erase all puzzle, best and play times.

## Rationale
Lets a device be handed to another player, or the owner re-test from scratch.

## Rules
- The reset needs a second, explicit confirmation inside the settings screen.
- Settings (difficulty, timer, sound) are kept.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — After a confirmed reset, every puzzle shows as New.
- **A2** — Cancelling the confirmation changes nothing.
