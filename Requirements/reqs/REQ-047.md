# REQ-047 — Finnish and English

## Statement
The game SHALL show every text, including puzzle titles, in Finnish WHEN the device language is Finnish, and in English otherwise.

## Rationale
The players are Finnish children aged 8 and up; the puzzle files already carry a Finnish title. The owner accepted the language proposal (SRC-011, G1). There is no in-game language switch: the device language decides.

## Rules
- Languages shipped: `fi` and `en`; any other device language gets `en`.
- Puzzle titles come from the puzzle file's `title[lang]`; validator rule V8 requires both `en` and `fi`.
- The size marks S, M and L (REQ-043) are the same in both languages.
- Pictures carry no text (REQ-039), so they need no translation.
- The free note (REQ-009) and the privacy text (REQ-049) exist in both languages.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — With the device in Finnish, the top bar, the settings screen and the buttons show Finnish; the Cat puzzle is titled "Kissa".
- **A2** — With the device in Swedish, everything is in English.

## Out of scope
More languages; a language switch inside the game.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: no in-game language switch.
