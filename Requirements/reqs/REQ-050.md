# REQ-050 — Finding a puzzle among many

## Statement
WHEN the player long-presses ›, the game SHALL show the next unsolved puzzle after the current one; WHEN the player presses the puzzle counter in the top bar, the game SHALL open a grid of every puzzle's silhouette, from which one press opens that puzzle.

## Rationale
With 20 or more puzzles (REQ-042) and only ‹ ›, finding the next unsolved puzzle takes many taps, and a player who "wants to finish them all" needs to see which are left. The owner accepted both aids (SRC-011, G4). Nothing is locked and no order is required (REQ-003).

## Rules
- Long press: at least 500 ms. IF every puzzle is solved, THEN the long press shows the next puzzle as a normal press would. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-011: the delay is the agent's)*
- The grid lists puzzles in the same order as ‹ › (REQ-040) and shows each puzzle's state: New as the flat silhouette, In progress as the silhouette with a dot, Solved as its picture.
- The grid is an overlay over the current puzzle; closing it (or the back button) returns to the same puzzle state.
- Each grid cell's touch area is at least 64 dp square (REQ-037). *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — With puzzles 3 and 7 unsolved and puzzle 3 open, a long press on › opens puzzle 7.
- **A2** — Pressing the counter shows one cell per puzzle, solved ones in colour.
- **A3** — Choosing a cell opens that puzzle without changing any other puzzle's state.

## Out of scope
Filtering or sorting by the player.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: solved cells show the picture at thumbnail size, not the piece layout (REQ-026 hides piece positions).
