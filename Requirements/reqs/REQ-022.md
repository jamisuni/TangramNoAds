# REQ-022 — Any exact cover solves the puzzle

## Statement
WHEN the last of the puzzle's pieces locks on the board, the game SHALL treat the puzzle as solved.

## Rationale
Every locked piece is inside the silhouette without overlap, and the pieces' total area equals the silhouette's area, so the locked pieces cover it exactly once all are placed. Alternative solutions count.

## Rules
- No comparison with the stored solution is made.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — An arrangement different from the stored solution that fills the silhouette counts as solved.
