# REQ-033 — Sound effects with an off switch

## Statement
The game SHALL play a short sound when a piece is picked up, turned, locked, returned to the tray and when a puzzle is solved, and SHALL play no sound WHILE sound is switched off in the settings.

## Rationale
Audible feedback confirms each action without text.

## Rules
- Each effect lasts at most 1 s.
- No sound signals failure: a return to the tray uses a soft low tone.
- A lock also gives a short haptic tick where the device supports it without a permission (REQ-010); the tick follows the sound switch. (SRC-011, D8)

## Acceptance
- **A1** — With sound off, no action makes a sound or a haptic tick.

## Out of scope
Music.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: sound is on by default.
