# REQ-041 — Warm-up puzzles first

## Statement
The game SHALL start its puzzle list, after the mini puzzles of REQ-045, with at least four warm-up puzzles of rating 1, in each of which at least half of every piece's outline lies on the silhouette's edge.

## Rationale
SRC-008: "add there few very easy ones to get easy start.. seriously these where too hard for me to solve fast to test application".

## Rules
- "At least half of every piece's outline on the silhouette edge" is the warm-up measure. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004: it is the agent's measure)*
- A warm-up is marked `kind: warmup` in its puzzle file; validator rule V12 checks the measure. (SRC-011, F7)
- Warm-ups may be abstract shapes (SRC-008).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — On a fresh install, the first four puzzles after the mini puzzles are warm-ups.
- **A2** — Each warm-up meets the half-outline measure.
