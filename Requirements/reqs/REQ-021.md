# REQ-021 — Landing preview

## Statement
WHILE the player drags a piece on Easy or Medium, the game SHALL draw a dashed outline at the position where the piece would lock if released now, and SHALL draw nothing when no position is valid.

## Rationale
Makes the locking rule predictable, so a drop never surprises the player. It shows a valid position, not the correct one.

## Rules
- Updated at most once per displayed frame.
- Not shown on Hard (TYPE-004).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Releasing the piece always locks it where the preview was drawn.
- **A2** — No preview is drawn on Hard.

## Out of scope
none

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: a white dashed outline, 2.5 dp wide, over the silhouette.
