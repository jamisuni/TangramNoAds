# REQ-003 — Free movement between puzzles

## Statement
The game SHALL let the player move to any puzzle at any time, and SHALL keep each puzzle's progress when the player leaves it.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "< left and right > scrolling to go from puzzle to another", "allow to move next one w simple click if cant solve", "If leaving puzzle half way can return it and restart if so wants or continue".

## Rules
- No puzzle is locked behind another puzzle.
- Each puzzle keeps its own state (TYPE-006).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — From any puzzle, every other puzzle can be reached without solving anything.
- **A2** — Leaving and returning to a puzzle never loses its state.

## Out of scope
Accounts, cloud sync, several player profiles.
