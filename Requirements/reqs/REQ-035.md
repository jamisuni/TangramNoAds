# REQ-035 — Phone portrait layout

## Statement
WHERE the layout class is Phone, the game SHALL run in portrait only and SHALL show from top to bottom: the top bar, the board, and the tray in two rows.

## Rationale
SRC-001: "normal phone on up position UI layout (bottom more narrow)".

## Rules
- Tray row 1 holds the large and medium triangles; row 2 holds the square, parallelogram and small triangles. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*
- The board takes all the height left, and the silhouette is fitted with about 10 % padding. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*
- The screen is drawn edge to edge behind the system bars; the tray stays clear of the gesture bar and every control respects the system insets. (SRC-011, G7)
- The Android back button closes an open overlay; otherwise it leaves the app normally, the state being already saved (REQ-025). (SRC-011, G7)

## Acceptance
- **A1** — On a 390 × 844 dp phone, all seven pieces are visible in the tray without scrolling.
- **A2** — With gesture navigation, no tray cell lies under the gesture bar.

## Out of scope
Phone landscape.
