# REQ-019 — Lock to corners and neighbours

## Statement
WHEN the player drops a piece on the board, the game SHALL move it to the best position within the lock distance where one of its corners coincides with a corner of the silhouette outline or of a piece already on the board, where it lies completely inside the silhouette, and where it overlaps no other piece.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "rules where it can tie up like only sides of puzzle and tieup to other pieces", "doesnt need exact millimeter correct locations". SRC-003: no support for placing a piece in the middle without neighbours, so there are no hidden lock points. Every arrangement a player builds is anchorable at every step: the uncovered region always has a convex corner, that corner is always an anchor, and the piece covering it must have a vertex there (SRC-011, F1).

## Rules
- Anchor points are only the silhouette's outline corners and the corners of pieces currently on the board.
- Lock distance, the choice among candidates and the geometric tolerance: TYPE-004.
- The turn and mirror the piece has when dropped are the ones tried; the lock never changes them.
- The lock animation lasts 180 ms. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A piece released within the lock distance of a valid position locks there exactly.
- **A2** — A locked piece never overlaps another piece and never sticks out of the silhouette.
- **A3** — A piece that touches no outline corner and has no neighbour to tie to never locks.
- **A4** — Six pieces locked in any arrangement that leaves a hole of the seventh piece's shape: the seventh piece locks into it.

## Out of scope
What happens when no position is valid (REQ-020).
