# REQ-019 — Lock to corners and neighbours

## Statement
WHEN the player drops a piece on the board, the game SHALL move it to the nearest position within the lock distance where one of its corners coincides with a corner of the silhouette outline or of a piece already on the board, where it lies completely inside the silhouette, and where it overlaps no other piece.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "rules where it can tie up like only sides of puzzle and tieup to other pieces", "doesnt need exact millimeter correct locations". SRC-003: no support for placing a piece in the middle without neighbours, so there are no hidden lock points.

## Rules
- Anchor points are only the silhouette's outline corners and the corners of pieces currently on the board.
- Lock distance per level: TYPE-004.
- Among valid positions, the nearest wins; a position where more corners meet anchors wins a tie. *(Inferred from SRC-004)*
- The lock animation lasts 180 ms. *(Inferred from SRC-004)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A piece released within the lock distance of a valid position locks there exactly.
- **A2** — A locked piece never overlaps another piece and never sticks out of the silhouette.
- **A3** — A piece that touches no outline corner and has no neighbour to tie to never locks.

## Out of scope
What happens when no position is valid (REQ-020).
