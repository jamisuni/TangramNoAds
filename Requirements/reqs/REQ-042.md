# REQ-042 — First-release puzzle count

## Statement
The first release SHALL ship at least 20 puzzles, over at least four themes, every one of them reviewed by a person (REQ-039), including the classic square.

## Rationale
Enough content to be worth installing, small enough for the owner to solve and approve each one. The owner accepted 20–25 puzzles for the first release and 40 for the second (SRC-011, D6).

## Rules
- Count: at least 20, at most what the owner has reviewed.
- Themes: at least four of the categories in ../Spec/03-puzzle-format.md.
- Mini and warm-up puzzles count toward the 20. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-011: the review counted the library as a whole)*

## Acceptance
- **A1** — The release contains at least 20 puzzles with reviewedByHuman: true.
- **A2** — At least four categories are present.
- **A3** — shapes-square is among them.

## Out of scope
The second release (target 40).
