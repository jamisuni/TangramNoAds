# REQ-026 — Solved puzzle shows its picture, with Retry

## Statement
WHILE a solved puzzle is shown, the game SHALL display only its picture, without piece positions, together with a Retry button, the best time and a Next button; WHEN the player presses Retry, the game SHALL start the puzzle again from an empty board.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "can see puzzle as solved but cant see anymore exact block positions.. and have retry".

## Rules
- Retry keeps the best time.
- The tray is replaced by the Retry, best time and Next row.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A solved puzzle never shows where the pieces were.
- **A2** — Retry leads to an empty silhouette with all pieces in the tray.
