# REQ-016 — Tap to turn 45°

## Statement
WHEN the player taps a piece in the tray, the game SHALL turn it 45° clockwise; WHEN the player taps a piece on the board, the game SHALL turn it 45° clockwise about its centre and keep the turn only if the turned piece locks within the lock distance by REQ-019.

## Rationale
The owner wants turning pieces (SRC-002: "I want to have turning pieces"). A one-finger way to turn also works one-handed on a phone and with a mouse. In round 6 the owner dropped the tray turn buttons because tapping does the same (SRC-011: "I can just press it and it spins"), so the tap is the way to turn a piece before dragging it.

## Rules
- One tap turns one step of TYPE-003.
- IF a turned board piece cannot lock near its place, THEN the game SHALL restore its previous turn and shake it once.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Eight taps return a tray piece to its starting turn.
- **A2** — A board piece that has no room to turn keeps its position and turn.

## Out of scope
Mirroring (REQ-018).
