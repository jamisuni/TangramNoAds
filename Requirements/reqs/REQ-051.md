# REQ-051 — The corners pulse once in a mini puzzle

## Statement
WHILE a mini puzzle (REQ-045) is shown, WHEN a dropped piece goes home (REQ-020), the game SHALL pulse the silhouette's outline corners once.

## Rationale
The first success metric in the vision is that children never ask "why won't it go there?" more than once. With no hidden anchors, a piece dropped on the middle of a straight side goes home and nothing explains why. A one-time pulse of the corners says "hold on to a corner" and reveals no solution, so the no-hints decision (Q10) stands. The owner accepted it (SRC-011, D5).

## Rules
- The pulse lasts about 600 ms and shows only the outline corners, never a piece position. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-011: the duration is the agent's)*
- It happens only in mini puzzles; full-set and warm-up puzzles never show anchors.
- With reduced motion enabled, the corners are shown for 600 ms without animation.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — In a mini puzzle, a drop in the middle of the silhouette sends the piece home and the corners pulse once.
- **A2** — In a warm-up, the same drop sends the piece home and nothing else is shown.

## Out of scope
Hints of any kind (Q10).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: 600 ms; the corner marker is a small circle in the accent colour.
