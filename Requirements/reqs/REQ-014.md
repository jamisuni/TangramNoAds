# REQ-014 — Drag and drop a piece

## Statement
WHEN the player drags a piece from the tray or the board, the game SHALL move it with the finger at its true board size, and WHEN the player releases it, the game SHALL resolve the drop by REQ-019 and REQ-020.

## Rationale
SRC-001: "kid can drag and drop them to shaped area". SRC-002: "drag + rotate + drop".

## Rules
- A tray piece grows from miniature to board size within 120 ms of the drag starting.
- The piece's size never changes on the board. SRC-009: "rule stays.. size cannot be scaled for piece".
- While dragged, the piece floats above the touch point so the finger does not hide it.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A dragged piece follows the finger without delay.
- **A2** — The piece is never drawn larger or smaller than its board size once on the board.

## Out of scope
What counts as a tap (REQ-015).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the floating offset puts the piece's lowest point 30 dp (phone) or 40 dp (tablet) above the finger.
