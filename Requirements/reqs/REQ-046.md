# REQ-046 — Passcode-protected solution reveal for testing

## Statement
WHERE the build is a prototype or test build, the game SHALL show a DEV button on the board that asks for the passcode 0417, and after the right passcode SHALL offer to show every piece's solution position on the silhouette and to solve the current puzzle at once; WHERE the build is a release build, the game SHALL NOT contain the DEV button or the solution reveal.

## Rationale
It lets the owner confirm that every puzzle of the library (REQ-007) really is solvable. SRC-010: "I need somekind of development time 'cheat' button that shows me how pieces supposed to go.. as I have hard time solving few of these to believe they work :D maybe press to bring msg box with passcode 0417 to show solution".

## Rules
- A wrong passcode shows "Wrong passcode." and reveals nothing.
- After the right passcode the aid stays unlocked until the page or app is restarted.
- The solution overlay draws each piece's stored solution position in its colour with its id, and stays on while the player browses puzzles until it is switched off.
- A puzzle solved by the aid is marked solved but does not set a best time.
- The DEV button is hidden on a solved puzzle and in the design sheets (`../Spec/ui/`).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Entering 1234 is refused; entering 0417 unlocks the aid.
- **A2** — "Show the solution" draws one shape per piece of the puzzle on the silhouette.
- **A3** — "Solve this puzzle now" shows the solved picture and leaves the best time empty.
- **A4** — A release build contains no DEV button.

## Out of scope
Hints for players (Q10 in ../Spec/05-open-questions.md: no hints).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the DEV button sits in the bottom-left corner of the board, small and dashed, so it is not mistaken for part of the game.
