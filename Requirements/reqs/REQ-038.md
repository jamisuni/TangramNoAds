# REQ-038 — Full-set, edge-first puzzles of any shape

## Statement
The game SHALL only include puzzles that use every piece of TYPE-001 exactly once, except mini puzzles (REQ-045), and that can be completed in an order where each piece locks on an outline corner or on a corner of an earlier piece.

## Rationale
SRC-003: "we do not support gift boxes.. nor we need suppor placing something middle without neighbors". SRC-008: "We can use random shapes … even rectangle is fine".

## Rules
- Every puzzle uses all seven pieces, except a puzzle marked mini. *(Inferred from SRC-004)*
- Pieces touch along edges; a join at a single corner point does not count as connected.
- Checked by the puzzle validator rules V1–V11 in ../tools/validate_puzzles.py.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Every puzzle file passes V1–V11.
- **A2** — Each puzzle's printed build order places all of its pieces.

## Out of scope
Other piece sets (idea #PieceSets).
