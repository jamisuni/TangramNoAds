# REQ-029 — Active play time: today and total

## Statement
WHILE the game counts an active second (TYPE-005), the game SHALL add it to today's and to the all-time play time, and the settings screen SHALL show both.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "total ACTIVE time on puzzle game"; SRC-003: "we keep tracking active playtime".

## Rules
- Today's time restarts at local midnight.
- Times are shown as m:ss below one hour, and as h and min from one hour up.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — Leaving the phone untouched for 5 minutes adds at most 60 s.
- **A2** — The settings screen shows today's and all-time play time.

## Out of scope
Charts or history per day.
