# REQ-017 — Two-finger twist in 45° steps

## Statement
WHILE the player drags a piece, WHEN a second finger touches the screen and rotates around the first, the game SHALL turn the piece one 45° step each time the twist angle passes a further 45° step boundary.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "maybe some fixed standard two finger spinning with predefined angles... 45 degrees?" (the owner's idea).

## Rules
- Step boundaries are at ±22.5°, ±67.5° and so on of twist from where the second finger started.
- Lifting the second finger keeps the turn reached; the drag continues.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A 90° twist turns the piece exactly two steps.
- **A2** — A 20° twist does not turn the piece.

## Out of scope
Free (non-stepped) rotation.

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: the twist works only while a piece is being dragged.
