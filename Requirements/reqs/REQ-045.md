# REQ-045 — Mini 3-piece puzzles

## Statement
The game SHALL start its puzzle list with at least one mini puzzle that uses exactly three pieces of TYPE-001, and WHILE a mini puzzle is open, the tray SHALL show only that puzzle's pieces.

## Rationale
SRC-009: "make me one even more simple with just 3 pieces to make something for fast to test 'solved' ones".

## Rules
- A mini puzzle is marked `"mini": true` in its puzzle file; validator rule V9 then accepts 1 to 6 distinct pieces instead of all seven.
- Every other rule for puzzles applies unchanged: edge-first buildable (V11), a solved picture, a rating (REQ-040).
- Mini puzzles have rating 1 and sort before the warm-ups.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — A fresh install opens on a mini puzzle whose tray holds three pieces.
- **A2** — Placing those three pieces shows the solved picture.

## Out of scope
Puzzles for other piece sets (idea #PieceSets).

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: two mini puzzles ship in the prototype (a triangle and a square) and they stay in the game as a first-run introduction; the owner asked for one, for testing.
