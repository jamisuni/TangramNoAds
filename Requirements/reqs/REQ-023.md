# REQ-023 — Solved picture

## Statement
WHEN a puzzle is solved, the game SHALL replace the coloured pieces with the puzzle's stylised picture, drawn with exactly the silhouette's outline.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "after it solved a picture changes to more stylish house look with exact same frame as 'solved' view".

## Rules
- The picture fades in over 800 ms, starting 600 ms after the last piece locks. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*
- A short celebration (a 4-note chime and confetti lasting 2.4 s) plays with it. *(Agent proposal, confirmed by the owner's lock SRC-014; first inferred from SRC-004)*
- WHERE the device asks for reduced motion, the pieces do not pop and no confetti is shown; the picture still appears. (SRC-011, G7)

## Acceptance
- **A1** — The picture's outline is identical to the silhouette's.
- **A2** — No piece boundary is visible in the picture.

## Out of scope
Animated pictures.
