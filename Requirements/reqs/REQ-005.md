# REQ-005 — Play-time tracking

## Statement
The game SHALL track the time used to solve each puzzle and the player's total active play time.

## Rationale
SRC-002: "keep track of per puzzle time used to solve plus total ACTIVE time". SRC-003: "we keep tracking active playtime".

## Rules
- Time is measured in active seconds (TYPE-005).

## Acceptance
- **A1** — After solving a puzzle, its solve time is available.
- **A2** — The total active play time is available at any time.

## Out of scope
Leaderboards, sharing, analytics (nothing leaves the device, REQ-010). Donations (dropped, SRC-003).
