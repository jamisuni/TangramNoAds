# REQ-037 — Touch target sizes

## Statement
The game SHALL give every interactive control a touch area of at least 48 dp in both directions.

## Rationale
Guidelines for children's touch targets (SRC-006) and measured tap inaccuracy (SRC-005).

## Rules
- ‹ and › are 52 dp; the flip button's touch area is 60 dp (REQ-018).
- Tray cells follow REQ-013.

## Acceptance
- **A1** — No control in any screen has a touch area below 48 dp.

## Out of scope
none

## Assumptions
- ASSUMPTION: 48 dp minimum (SRC-006 suggests 2 cm, about 80 dp, for under-9s; our players are 8+).
