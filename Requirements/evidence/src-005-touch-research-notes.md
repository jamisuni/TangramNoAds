# SRC-005 — Extraction notes: touch interaction for children aged 3 to 6

Source: Vatavu, Cramariuc, Schipor, *International Journal of Human-Computer Studies*, 2015.
https://mintviz.usv.ro/publications/ijhcs2015.pdf (read 2026-09-27).

| Claim taken | Where in the paper | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| Tap miss distance is 4.5 mm at age 3 and 3.4 mm at age 5 | results, tap task | touch areas larger than the drawn shape |
| Young children may hold a "tap" for up to about 5 s | guideline on tap duration | a tap is defined by movement, not duration |
| Single-touch success is about 92 %; bimanual multi-touch about 54 % | results, drag and multi-touch tasks | two fingers are never *required* |
| Drag success rises with age (73.5 % under 4, 89.2 % over 5) | results, drag task | a failed drop must cost nothing |
| Guidelines: avoid required multi-touch; account for the finger hiding the target | design guidelines 8 and 9 | a dragged piece floats above the finger |

**Ambiguity:** the subjects are 3–6 years old; our players are 8+. The
numbers are upper bounds on difficulty, not targets. Every value the REQs
use is an agent ASSUMPTION.
