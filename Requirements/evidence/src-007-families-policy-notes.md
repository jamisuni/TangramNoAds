# SRC-007 — Extraction notes: Google Play Families policies

Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9893335 (read 2026-09-27).

| Obligation taken | Used by |
|---|---|
| Apps whose audience includes children must disclose any collection of personal information and must not transmit device identifiers such as the advertising ID | the "nothing leaves the device" rule |
| Ads, when present, must come from certified SDKs and must not be personalised | not applicable: there are no ads |
| The Play Console target-audience and Data safety declarations must be accurate | the release checklist (the "no data collected" answer) |

**Ambiguity:** the policy changes over time; re-check it before the first release.
