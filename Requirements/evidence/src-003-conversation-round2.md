## Notes

- The quoted sentence is the agent's own explanation of why it had added
  hidden lock points (the solution's corner points). The owner's decisions:
  - Pieces are **not** supported in places where nothing ties them (no
    neighbour, no outline corner), so no hidden lock points.
  - Puzzles like the "gift box" (the classic square, where a piece can
    touch the outline only in the middle of a side) are not supported.
  - Donations are dropped completely.
  - Active play-time tracking stays.
  - The product theme: **"Enjoy, it's absolutely free."**
