# Features — TangramNoAds

## #Promise — Enjoy, it's absolutely free · locked
No money of any kind and nothing that leaves the device.

### #NoMoney — No ads, purchases or money asks · locked
The game never shows ads, sells anything or asks for money.

### #DeviceOnly — Everything stays on the device · locked
No network use; progress and play time are stored locally.

## #Solving — Solving a puzzle · locked
Build the shown silhouette from the seven pieces by dragging and turning them.

### #Board — Silhouette board · locked
The target is an empty, flat silhouette; drop places are never shown.

### #Tray — Piece tray · locked
The puzzle's pieces wait as miniatures at the bottom, always in the same order.

### #SizeMarks — Size marks in the tray · locked
The three triangle sizes carry S, M and L marks, so the player knows how big a piece will be.

### #Drag — Drag and drop a piece · locked
Drag a piece from the tray or the board; it shows at its true size on the board.

### #Turning — Turning pieces · locked
Pieces turn in 45° steps; the parallelogram can also be mirrored.

#### #TapTurn — Tap to turn · locked
A tap turns the piece one step, in the tray or on the board.

#### #TwistTurn — Two-finger twist · locked
While dragging, a second finger twists the piece in steps.

#### #TurnButtons — Turn buttons in the tray · locked
Withdrawn 2026-09-28 (REQ-044): tapping a tray piece turns it, so the ↺ ↻ buttons went.

#### #Flip — Mirror the parallelogram · locked
A flip badge next to the parallelogram mirrors it; the badge is always shown.

### #Locking — Pieces lock or go home · locked
A dropped piece ties to the shape's corners or to other pieces, or returns to the tray.

#### #AnchorLock — Lock to corners and neighbours · locked
No millimetre aiming: the best valid spot near the drop wins.

#### #GoHome — Missed drop returns to the tray · locked
No loose pieces ever lie on the board.

#### #LandingPreview — Landing preview · locked
While dragging, a dashed outline shows where the piece would lock.

#### #CornerTeach — The corners pulse once · locked
In a mini puzzle, a drop that goes home makes the silhouette's corners pulse once: pieces hold on to corners.

### #SolvedCheck — Any exact cover solves · locked
All the puzzle's pieces locked means solved, whatever arrangement the player found.

### #SolvedPicture — Solved picture · locked
On solving, the pieces turn into a stylised picture with exactly the same outline.

## #Browsing — Moving between puzzles · locked
Free movement between puzzles; nothing is locked and nothing is lost.

### #PuzzleNav — Previous and next puzzle · locked
‹ and › at the top of the screen; › also skips an unsolved puzzle; the list wraps.

### #ResumePuzzle — Continue or restart · locked
A half-done puzzle keeps its pieces; the player continues or restarts it.

### #SolvedView — Solved puzzle with retry · locked
A solved puzzle shows only its picture, with Retry.

### #PuzzleOverview — Finding a puzzle among many · locked
Long-press › for the next unsolved puzzle; the counter opens a grid of all silhouettes.

## #PlayTime — Play time · locked
Per-puzzle solve time and total active play time.

### #ActiveTime — Active play time · locked
Today's and all-time active play time, shown in the settings.

### #PuzzleTime — Solve time and best time · locked
Each puzzle counts its own active time and remembers the best solve.

### #TimerDisplay — Timer on screen · locked
An optional running timer on the board, off by default.

## #Settings — Settings · locked
One settings screen opened from the top bar.

### #SettingsMenu — Open the settings · locked
A gear in the top bar opens timer, sound, play time, reset, how to play, the free note and the privacy text.

### #SoundToggle — Sound effects · locked
Short sounds for picking, locking, returning and solving; can be switched off.

### #ResetProgress — Reset all progress · locked
Erase all solved puzzles and times after a confirmation step.

### #Language — Finnish and English · locked
The game speaks Finnish or English, following the device language.

## #Layout — Phone and tablet · locked
One game, laid out for phones in portrait and for tablets.

### #PhoneLayout — Phone portrait layout · locked
Narrow screens: portrait only, with the tray at the bottom in two rows.

### #TabletLayout — Tablet layout · locked
Wide screens: both orientations, with one wide tray row.

### #TouchTargets — Touch target sizes · locked
Every player control is large enough for school-age fingers.

## #Content — Puzzle content · locked
The puzzles: full-set silhouettes, figures or abstract shapes, each with its picture.

### #PuzzleShapes — Full-set puzzles · locked
Every puzzle uses all seven pieces (mini puzzles excepted) and is a valid tangram; any shape will do.

### #PuzzleMini — Mini 3-piece puzzles · locked
A few tiny puzzles that use only some pieces, for a fast first success and for testing.

### #PuzzleWarmups — Warm-up puzzles first · locked
After the mini puzzles, the library opens with puzzles where each piece's shape shows in the silhouette.

### #PuzzleArt — Picture for every puzzle · locked
Each puzzle carries the stylised picture shown when it is solved.

### #PuzzleOrder — Puzzle difficulty and order · locked
Puzzles are listed by kind, then rated 1–5 from easiest to hardest.

### #PuzzleVolume — First-version puzzle count · locked
At least 20 hand-checked puzzles over four themes, including the classic square.

## #DevTools — Developer aids · locked

### #SolutionReveal — Show the solution with a passcode · locked

## #PieceSets — Other piece sets · idea
