# Features — TangramNoAds

## #Promise — Enjoy, it's absolutely free · ai-approved
No money of any kind and nothing that leaves the device.

### #NoMoney — No ads, purchases or money asks · ai-approved
The game never shows ads, sells anything or asks for money.

### #DeviceOnly — Everything stays on the device · ai-approved
No network use; progress and play time are stored locally.

## #Solving — Solving a puzzle · ai-approved
Build the shown silhouette from the seven pieces by dragging and turning them.

### #Board — Silhouette board · ai-approved
The target is an empty, flat silhouette; drop places are never shown.

### #Tray — Piece tray · ai-approved
The puzzle's pieces wait as miniatures at the bottom, always in the same order.

### #SizeMarks — Size marks in the tray · ai-approved
The three triangle sizes carry S, M and L marks, so the player knows how big a piece will be.

### #Drag — Drag and drop a piece · ai-approved
Drag a piece from the tray or the board; it shows at its true size on the board.

### #Turning — Turning pieces · ai-approved
Pieces turn in 45° steps; the parallelogram can also be mirrored.

#### #TapTurn — Tap to turn · ai-approved
A tap turns the piece one step.

#### #TwistTurn — Two-finger twist · ai-approved
While dragging, a second finger twists the piece in steps.

#### #TurnButtons — Turn buttons in the tray · ai-approved
Small ↺ ↻ buttons in each tray cell turn the piece before it is dragged.

#### #Flip — Mirror the parallelogram · ai-approved
On Hard the player mirrors the parallelogram with a flip button.

### #Locking — Pieces lock or go home · ai-approved
A dropped piece ties to the shape's corners or to other pieces, or returns to the tray.

#### #AnchorLock — Lock to corners and neighbours · ai-approved
No millimetre aiming: the nearest valid spot near the drop wins.

#### #GoHome — Missed drop returns to the tray · ai-approved
No loose pieces ever lie on the board.

#### #LandingPreview — Landing preview · ai-approved
While dragging on Easy and Medium, a dashed outline shows where the piece would lock.

### #SolvedCheck — Any exact cover solves · ai-approved
All the puzzle's pieces locked means solved, whatever arrangement the player found.

### #SolvedPicture — Solved picture · ai-approved
On solving, the pieces turn into a stylised picture with exactly the same outline.

## #Browsing — Moving between puzzles · ai-approved
Free movement between puzzles; nothing is locked and nothing is lost.

### #PuzzleNav — Previous and next puzzle · ai-approved
‹ and › at the top of the screen; › also skips an unsolved puzzle.

### #ResumePuzzle — Continue or restart · ai-approved
A half-done puzzle keeps its pieces; the player continues or restarts it.

### #SolvedView — Solved puzzle with retry · ai-approved
A solved puzzle shows only its picture, with Retry.

## #Difficulty — Easy, Medium, Hard · ai-approved
Three difficulty levels for school-age players and up.

### #DifficultyChoice — Choose the difficulty · ai-approved
The player picks Easy, Medium or Hard in the settings.

### #DifficultyRules — What a level changes · ai-approved
The level changes how forgiving the controls are, not which puzzles exist.

## #PlayTime — Play time · ai-approved
Per-puzzle solve time and total active play time.

### #ActiveTime — Active play time · ai-approved
Today's and all-time active play time, shown in the settings.

### #PuzzleTime — Solve time and best time · ai-approved
Each puzzle counts its own active time and remembers the best solve.

### #TimerDisplay — Timer on screen · ai-approved
An optional running timer on the board.

## #Settings — Settings · ai-approved
One settings screen opened from the top bar.

### #SettingsMenu — Open the settings · ai-approved
A gear in the top bar opens difficulty, timer, sound, play time and reset.

### #SoundToggle — Sound effects · ai-approved
Short sounds for picking, locking, returning and solving; can be switched off.

### #ResetProgress — Reset all progress · ai-approved
Erase all solved puzzles and times after a confirmation step.

## #Layout — Phone and tablet · ai-approved
One game, laid out for phones in portrait and for tablets.

### #PhoneLayout — Phone portrait layout · ai-approved
Narrow screens: portrait only, with the tray at the bottom in two rows.

### #TabletLayout — Tablet layout · ai-approved
Wide screens: both orientations, with one wide tray row.

### #TouchTargets — Touch target sizes · ai-approved
Every control is large enough for school-age fingers.

## #Content — Puzzle content · captured
The puzzles: full-set silhouettes of recognisable figures, each with its picture.

### #PuzzleShapes — Full-set, edge-first puzzles · ai-approved
Every puzzle uses all seven pieces (mini puzzles excepted) and can be built from the edges inwards; any shape will do.

### #PuzzleMini — Mini 3-piece puzzles · ai-approved
A few tiny puzzles that use only some pieces, for a fast first success and for testing.

### #PuzzleWarmups — Warm-up puzzles first · ai-approved
After the mini puzzles, the library opens with puzzles where each piece's shape shows in the silhouette.

### #PuzzleArt — Picture for every puzzle · ai-approved
Each puzzle carries the stylised picture shown when it is solved.

### #PuzzleOrder — Puzzle difficulty and order · ai-approved
Puzzles are rated 1–5 and listed from easiest to hardest.

### #PuzzleVolume — First-version puzzle count · captured
How many puzzles the first release ships with.
OQ: how many puzzles, and which themes, does the first release need?

## #PieceSets — Other piece sets · idea
Later modes with non-traditional sets that are not the classic seven pieces.

## #Accessibility — Colour-blind support · idea
Pieces distinguishable without relying on colour alone.
