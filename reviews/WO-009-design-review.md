## Verdict

**recirculate → design-author (rev 1, then a spot-check).**

Most of the design is right, and should be kept:
- WO-009 really needs no product `src/main` change.
- No signing material enters the repository.
- The AI never sets the flag.
- The release library stays equal to `Tangrams/` (G-08).
- V-09 stays out of `gradlew test`.
- Every theme ends with at least two puzzles, so a rejected theme cannot break REQ-042 A2.

The author named the highest risk honestly. But the section the author flagged does not hold. **The token placement is coverage theater (B1).**
- `REQ-042.A1` and `REQ-039.A2` sit on a rule that is written in the test and proven on the test's own fixtures. In every default run, the real library is never asserted.
- `REQ-048.A1` goes green while the privacy URL reads `TBD-OWNER`.
- `REQ-048.A2` and `REQ-001.A2` are green on text the AI typed itself.
- A green trace-check would then mean something different for these five IDs than for every other ID in the project. DA-156 makes that change as a row-12 "ambiguity", but it is a reading of a SWDev traceability rule, which row 13 keeps human.

The design also misses a store fact that decides the first Console step. **Google Play's listing rules bar "free" from app titles (B2).** REQ-048's own title, "Tangram, absolutely free", is therefore very likely unpublishable on the channel the design recommends. That is a locked-REQ conflict (row 14, F1) and belongs at the top of the owner's list, not in a test that pins it.

Nine Shoulds follow:
- **S1:** the first task's own done-check goes red.
- **S2:** the library-size ripples are incomplete.
- **S3:** a locked picture rule is left as "information" while existing pictures appear to break it.
- **S4:** the review-record seams have three gaps.
- **S5:** there is no path for a rejected puzzle.
- **S6:** the store-bound artifact chain is not closed.
- **S7:** the hosted privacy text is over-constrained.
- **S8:** the gate's pass path is never exercised on real files.
- **S9:** the owner-fork list has gaps.

**2 Blockers, 9 Shoulds, 11 Notes.**

## Trajectory & quality

- **Verification actually run?** The author read the right inputs and checked the content packaging, the schema enum, `Puzzle.reviewedByHuman`, the verifier arguments and the trace-check globs. Two claims were narrated rather than checked:

- **Path sane?** Yes. It is rev 0, with an honest risk flag that asks for exactly this challenge.
