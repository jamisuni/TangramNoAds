**Status:** **Closed 2026-10-04** (G3 passed — governance row 7 = ai; checkpoint 7 surfaced to Jami)  ·  **Mode:** Full

## Goal

A settings screen opened from the top bar. Opening and closing it changes nothing on the board. It
has a sound switch: when sound is off, no action makes a sound or a haptic tick. It has a reset of all
progress, with a confirmation: confirmed, every puzzle is New; cancelled, nothing changes. It shows
the "Enjoy, it's absolutely free" note and the privacy text in full. There is no difficulty control.
The soft return sound and the lock tick from WO-001's carry arrive here, with the switch that silences them.

## Mode justification

Chosen: **Full**. A new module (`settings`), audio and haptics (a no-permission constraint: REQ-010 A2 and V-08 fail on any
permission element), a destructive action (reset) on the frozen store, and many carried checks.

## Constraints & notes

- **No permission at all** (REQ-010 A2, V-08). Haptics must use a path that needs no `VIBRATE` permission, for example
  `View.performHapticFeedback`. Sounds must be generated or shipped without any network, and any audio asset needs a
  clear, free licence. Prefer synthesis in code.
- **The v1 save format is frozen** (LOCK-V1, G-09). The sound setting and any new persisted value need a home that is
  not the frozen file, or else a designed migration (row 11).
- **Device channels (AGENTS.md):** JVM; device on `Medium_Phone_API_37.0` **and** `Phone_API_26`, one at a time;
  `tools/device_reset.py` before and after every device step; V-04 + V-08 at the close.
- **Strings:** fi + en (V-05). The REQ-009 note and the REQ-049 privacy text are locked texts: use them verbatim from the REQs.
  AI-written Finnish goes on the owner list.
- **Drawing (DA-92):** `play` draws every path in px. Never draw a path under a canvas scale.
- **Lessons (AGENTS.md):**
  - every frozen seam names its delivering task;
  - adapters fail loudly;
  - Compose test imports;
  - compile staged and held-out tests early;
  - the walk measures layout bounds (DA-109);
  - never put a backslash in a Python literal that writes a doc.
