## Goal

Make the game releasable as promised. The store-bound release artifact keeps the free promise: no ads, no purchases, no permission and no network. The first release ships a library that meets REQ-042: at least 20 puzzles, at least four themes, the classic square included, and every puzzle reviewed by a person. A release checklist collects the manual evidence that tests cannot give (decision F17): the store listing's labels, title and privacy link, and the human review of every puzzle.
