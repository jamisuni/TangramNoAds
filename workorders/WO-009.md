## Goal

Make the game releasable as promised. The store-bound release artifact keeps the free promise: no ads, no purchases, no permission and no network. The first release ships a library that meets REQ-042: at least 20 puzzles, at least four themes, the classic square included, and every puzzle reviewed by a person. A release checklist collects the manual evidence that tests cannot give (decision F17): the store listing's labels, title and privacy link, and the human review of every puzzle.

## Owner floors and human rows (expected; the design confirms)

- **`reviewedByHuman` is never set by the AI** (AGENTS; REQ-039 A2, REQ-042 A1). Only Jami's review sets it. The AI prepares the review material.
- **Publishing to a store, the store account, the listing text, and the choice of store channel are the owner's.** These are outward-facing and human, and the channel is still open (F17, index §5). The AI drafts; Jami decides and acts.
- **The release signing key** (security floor) is Jami's. The AI never creates or handles a production key, and uses throwaway signing for tests only.
