## Goal

The game measures **active** play time only. Today's and all-time play time count every in-app second while the
player is active, browsing and settings included, and stop shortly after the last touch (REQ-029 with TYPE-005; owner
answer 2026-10-05). A puzzle's own time counts only while that puzzle is in play: never while the phone lies untouched,
the player browses away or the settings screen is open (REQ-030/031). Each puzzle gets a solve time and keeps its
fastest time as the best time. The settings screen shows today's and all-time play time. An optional on-screen timer,
off on a fresh install, shows the active seconds of the current puzzle.

## Constraints & notes

- **No wall-clock in tests.** Time must come from an injectable clock, as in WO-003's design (E5: the frame clock is handed in). Device tests use a controlled clock; nothing sleeps for minutes.
- **Local midnight** for "today" (DEF-001, REQ-029); the v1 `PlayTime.day` is the local date.
- **Best time** only from a player's own solve, never from an aid solve (REQ-030 rule, WO-005 DA-73/77). Slower re-solves keep the faster best (REQ-030 A2).
- **No permission, no network** (REQ-010, V-08). No alarm or background service is needed for counting time.
- **Device channels (AGENTS.md):**
  - API 37 and API 26, one emulator at a time;
  - `tools/device_reset.py` before and after every step;
  - V-04 + V-08 (incl. the feedback-caller check) at the close.
- **Lessons (AGENTS.md):**
  - every frozen seam names its delivering task;
  - adapters fail loudly;
  - Compose `assert…` helpers need imports;
  - compile staged and held-out tests early;
  - cross-file tests land together;
  - **solve by touch with the `solveByTouch` pattern, which the held kit carries too**;
  - `--rerun` goes after each Gradle task;
  - never put a backslash in a Python literal that writes a doc;
  - draw in px (DA-92).
