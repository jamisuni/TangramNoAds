**Status:** In progress (G3 passed — governance row 7 = ai)  ·  **Mode:** Full

## Goal

Build the lock engine the whole game rests on: exact piece geometry in the
kernel (TYPE-001, TYPE-003), the TYPE-004 lock search, and the `play`
module's drop resolution — a dropped piece locks at the best anchored spot or
goes home, the landing preview shows exactly where a release would lock, and
in a mini puzzle a missed drop makes the outline corners pulse once.
