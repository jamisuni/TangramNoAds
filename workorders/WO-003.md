## Goal

Put a real puzzle on a phone screen and make it solvable: the silhouette
board, the tray with size marks, dragging, turning (tap, two-finger twist,
flip badge), locking through the WO-001 engine, the solved check and the
solved picture — inside a minimal app shell (edge-to-edge, portrait on
phones, the layout class). **The first debug APK Jami can play.**
