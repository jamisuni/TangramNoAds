# 07 · Active play time (donations dropped)

*Study note. Round 2 decision (2026-09-27): **no donations, no support card, no money at all.** The theme is **"Enjoy, it's absolutely free."** Active play time is still tracked, for the player's own interest.*

## 1. What is tracked (on the device only)
| Measure | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Active second** | The app is visible **and** the last touch was ≤ 60 s ago. A phone left on the table does not count. |
| **Puzzle time** | Active seconds while that puzzle is on screen, unsolved, and has been touched (so just browsing past does not start it). It pauses while browsing away or in Settings and resumes on return. Restart resets it. |
| **Best time** | The shortest completed puzzle time. Shown on the solved screen. |
| **Today** | Active seconds since local midnight. |
| **Total** | All-time active seconds. |

Where it is shown: Settings → Play time (today, total, puzzles solved, best time per puzzle); the solved bar (best time); optionally a timer on the board (setting: Hard only / Always / Never).

Nothing leaves the device and there is no account or network, so the Google Play Data safety answer stays "No data collected".

## 2. Why donations were dropped (for the record)
- Jami's decision: neither of us needs money from this game; the point is that it is simply free.
- Background found during the study: Google Play only allows in-app donations through Play Billing, unless they go to a validated tax-exempt charity. In August–September 2026 Google made AnkiDroid remove its donation link. A tip button would also have put an "In-app purchases" label on the store listing. Dropping donations avoids all of this.

## 3. How "absolutely free" shows in the product
- Settings has a small green note: *"Enjoy, it's absolutely free. No ads, no purchases, no network. Your play time stays on this device."*
- The store listing leads with the same sentence.
- Nothing in the game ever asks for money, a rating, an account or permissions.
